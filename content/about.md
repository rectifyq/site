Rectifyq is **not** a commercial entity, a formal NGO, or a massive community hub. It is a personal initiative dedicated to continuous learning, skill refinement, and contributing back to the Malaysian cybersecurity ecosystem. The project is entirely self-funded (though support and sponsorships are always welcome to keep the lights on).

## The Malaysian Gap

While global threat intelligence is abundant regarding Western infrastructures, local organizations face a distinct gap:

1. **Localized Intelligence** — global APT reports rarely focus on Malaysia. Are we under-reported, or just under-observed?
2. **Budget Justification** — finding concrete, local references to justify cybersecurity spend to stakeholders and Boards.
3. **Actionable Contribution** — many talented Malaysians want to contribute to the local landscape but lack a focused platform or structured data to start.

Rectifyq exists to bridge the visibility gap between internal telemetry and the Malaysian threat landscape.

## How we work

- **Compile and Curate:** ingest and tag threat data with a strict focus on the Malaysian threat landscape.
- **Contextual Analysis:** we don't just share links — we analyze how global trends specifically impact the Malaysian context.
- **Enable the Community:** take our analysis, extend it, showcase it on your own platforms. Tag us and we'll amplify your work — see [[contribute|Contribute]].
