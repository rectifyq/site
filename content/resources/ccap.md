The question is never "which cert is best" — it's **"which cert moves *your* next step, at a price that makes sense in ringgit"**. Exchange rates make US-priced certs a serious investment for Malaysian practitioners; sequence deliberately.

| Career stage | Sensible targets | Notes for MY context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student / entry | Security+, eJPT, BTL1 | HR-recognized floor + hands-on proof; watch for student pricing |
| SOC analyst | CySA+, BTL1/BTL2, vendor SIEM certs | Blue-team signal that matches actual MY job ads |
| CTI-specific | GCTI, CTIA, arcX CTI, MAD20 | GCTI is the gold standard and priced like it — the others build the same muscles cheaper |
| Offensive | eCPPT → OSCP | OSCP remains the recognition king in MY hiring |
| Leadership | CISSP, CISM | For the CISO track; experience requirements are real |

## The action-plan part

1. **Pick by job ad, not by hype:** pull ten Malaysian postings for your target role and count which certs actually appear.
2. **One cert per stage, projects between:** a PhishHuntMY write-up plus one cert beats three certs and no portfolio — [[phishhuntmy/index|start here]].
3. **Employer funding:** HRD Corp-claimable trainings exist for several tracks — ask before self-funding.
