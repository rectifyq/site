The honest framing: most Malaysian teams below enterprise size can run a credible CTI function on **free tiers plus community feeds** — the paid question is about scale, SLA, and dark-web reach, not basic capability.

| Tier | What it covers | Examples to compare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free & community** | IoC enrichment, sandboxing, scan data, MY-local feeds | VirusTotal (free), urlscan.io, AlienVault OTX, Shodan (free), abuse.ch family, **Rectifyq feeds** |
| **Prosumer / low-cost** | Higher API quotas, monitoring alerts | Shodan membership, urlscan Pro tiers, small-team sandbox plans |
| **Commercial intel** | Curated reporting, dark-web/leak coverage, RFIs, SLAs | The big-vendor platforms — evaluate against your PIRs, not their demos |

## How to decide (the MY reality check)

1. Write your [[intel-program/pir|PIRs]] first — a subscription that doesn't serve a named requirement is a logo, not a capability.
2. Exhaust the free tier before the invoice: most teams discover quota, not features, is their real constraint.
3. Price in analyst time: a cheap feed nobody triages costs more than an expensive one that saves hours.
