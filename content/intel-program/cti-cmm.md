[CTI-CMM](https://cti-cmm.org) is the community-driven, vendor-neutral maturity model for CTI programs — built by 30+ practitioners, aligned with the C2M2's stakeholder domains, and use-case-driven rather than checkbox-driven. Version 1.2 spans **11 stakeholder domains** with maturity levels **CTI0 (pre-foundational) → CTI3 (leading)**.

We score ourselves against it publicly — **including the low scores**. A solo initiative claiming full maturity would be lying; showing the gaps is the point.

## Self-assessment — 2026 (draft)
